Revised and resubmitted Economic and Political Weekly·Sole author·2026

Reform by Stealth: A Three-Month Assessment of India's Labour Codes

India's four labour codes consolidate 29 central laws. I argue that they recalibrate the balance between state, capital and labour rather than simply modernise it. Using Annual Survey of Industries and Periodic Labour Force Survey data with a comparison of central and state rules, I show that raising the retrenchment approval threshold from 100 to 300 workers leaves roughly nine in ten industrial units outside routine government scrutiny.

Environmental Regime Uncertainty: measuring firm exposure to extraterritorial environmental regulation

A news text based index of regulatory uncertainty, in the spirit of Baker, Bloom and Davis, tracking how firms in Brazil and Indonesia are exposed to the EU Deforestation Regulation. I build the firm by sector panels in R and support difference in differences and event study analysis linking uncertainty to exports, filings and market reactions.

Policy commentaryEconomic and Political Weekly·2026

Why India's labour codes are reform by stealth

The codes replace labour inspectors with Inspector cum Facilitators, shift enforcement from deterrence to self certification, and recognise gig workers without giving them enforceable rights on wages, hours or algorithmic transparency. With central rules still in draft and most states yet to notify final rules, the details will decide whether this is modernisation or erosion.
