For Research Assistants

  • EconJobMarket: the central market for research assistantships, pre docs, and doctoral fellowships in economics.
  • PREDOC Opportunities: predoctoral research positions across economics and quantitative social science.
  • NBER Research Assistant Positions: a listing of research assistant openings with NBER affiliated economists outside the NBER.

For Applying to Graduate School

  • EconGradAdvice: practical advice on applying to economics PhD programmes.

Coding & Empirical Methods

  • Code and Data for the Social Sciences (Gentzkow and Shapiro): a practitioner's guide to organising reproducible research projects.
  • R for Data Science (Wickham and Grolemund): the standard introduction to data analysis with the tidyverse in R.
  • Data Science for Economists (Grant McDermott, EC607): lecture notes covering R, version control, and the modern empirical workflow.
  • IPA Data Cleaning Guide: Innovations for Poverty Action's standards for cleaning survey data reproducibly.
