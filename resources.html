The research assistant market

Where predoctoral and research assistant positions in economics are posted.

  • EconJobMarketeconjobmarket.orgThe central market for research assistantships, predoctoral positions and doctoral fellowships in economics.
  • PREDOC Opportunitiespredoc.orgPredoctoral research positions across economics and quantitative social science, with application timelines.
  • NBER research assistant positionsnber.orgResearch assistant openings with NBER affiliated economists outside the NBER itself.
  • AEA Job Openings for Economistsaeaweb.orgThe American Economic Association's listings, including research and predoctoral posts alongside faculty jobs.

Applying to graduate school

Honest, practical advice on PhD applications in economics.

  • EconGradAdvicesites.google.comPractical advice on applying to economics PhD programmes, from preparation to choosing an offer.
  • Econ PhD Advicegavinjqu.github.ioA community maintained, open source collection of resources for graduate students in economics and undergraduates considering a PhD.

Coding and empirical methods

How to organise a project, write code that others can run, and think about identification.

  • Code and Data for the Social Sciencesstanford.eduGentzkow and Shapiro's guide to organising reproducible research projects. Short, and worth rereading every year.
  • R for Data Sciencer4ds.hadley.nzWickham, Çetinkaya-Rundel and Grolemund's standard introduction to data analysis with the tidyverse in R.
  • Data Science for Economistsgithub.comGrant McDermott's EC607 lecture notes covering R, version control and the modern empirical workflow.
  • IPA data cleaning guidepovertyaction.github.ioInnovations for Poverty Action's standards for cleaning survey data reproducibly.
  • The Effecttheeffectbook.netNick Huntington-Klein's free textbook on research design and causal inference, with code in R, Stata and Python.
  • Causal Inference: The Mixtapemixtape.scunning.comScott Cunningham's free textbook on the main quasi experimental designs, with worked code.

Data I use

Public portals behind the papers on this site.

  • eSankhyiki (MoSPI)mospi.gov.inIndia's official statistics portal, including Periodic Labour Force Survey bulletins and Annual Survey of Industries results.
  • IPUMS CPSipums.orgHarmonised Current Population Survey microdata for the United States, used in my dissertation.
  • O*NET OnLineonetonline.orgOccupational task and work context data, the basis for classifying which jobs can be done remotely.
